02 — area guide

Angel & Islington

N1 / N7 · zone 1–2
the essentials

Leafy, a little polished, reliably good for brunch. Northern line, Victoria line and the Overground all within reach.

Chapel Market

Daily street market for fruit, veg, cheap eats and bargains — a proper local market, not a tourist one.

Camden Passage

Cobbled arcade of antique shops and vintage jewellery, with a market on Wednesdays and Saturdays.

Union Chapel

A working church turned gig venue — one of London's most atmospheric places to see live music.

Upper Street

The main social strip — over a hundred restaurants and bars, plus the Screen on the Green indie cinema.

Regent's Canal towpath

Runs along the edge of Islington — a good route for an evening walk or run.

Angel & Highbury & Islington

Northern line at Angel; Victoria line, Overground and National Rail at Highbury & Islington.

The vibe: wine bars, indie boutiques, and a 15-minute Northern line hop to Kings Cross.
03 — area guide

Camden

NW1 / NW5 · zone 2
the essentials

As you will already know...louder, a little scrappier, more character. Alt-fashion and live-music, with the canal running straight through the middle.

Camden Market

Stalls, vintage, street food and crafts across Camden Lock, Stables Market and Hawley Wharf — the 4th most-visited attraction in London.

Camden Lock & Regent's Canal

Canal boats and towpath cafes — a proper watch-the-barges-go-by kind of afternoon.

The Roundhouse & Electric Ballroom

Two of London's most iconic live music venues, both a short walk from the tube.

Primrose Hill

A 15-minute walk gets you one of the best skyline views in London.

Camden High Street

Alt-fashion, tattoo studios and cafes, with the Amy Winehouse statue tucked into the market.

Camden Town & Camden Road

Northern line at Camden Town; London Overground at Camden Road.

The vibe: markets on your doorstep, a touch further from Kings Cross than Angel but still very central.
